About NORVEX

Over 18 years of experience in lubricant manufacturing and export.

Who We Are

Founded in 2008, NORVEX Lubricants Co., Ltd. is an integrated lubricant manufacturer specializing in the research, production and export of automotive and industrial lubricants.

Our modern blending plant covers 45,000 square meters and is equipped with automatic blending systems, filling lines and a fully equipped quality control laboratory. With an annual capacity of 80,000 tons, we supply distributors, workshops, fleets and industrial users in more than 60 countries across the Middle East, Africa, Southeast Asia, South America and Eastern Europe.

We source premium Group II, Group III and PAO base oils and additives from internationally recognized suppliers, ensuring every product meets or exceeds industry specifications.
